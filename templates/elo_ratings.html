International Elo Ratings

Historical performance-based rankings for international football teams.

Underlying Formula

The Elo rating system calculates the relative skill levels of teams. After every match, ratings are updated based on the result and the expected outcome:

NewRating = OldRating + K * (Actual - Expected)

Where:

  • K: The development coefficient (set to 30), determining how drastically ratings change.
  • Actual: 1 for a win, 0.5 for a draw, 0 for a loss.
  • Expected: The win probability based on the rating difference: 1 / (1 + 10^((RatingOpponent - RatingTeam) / 400))

A Home Advantage of 100 points is added to the home team's rating (unless the match is neutral) for the purpose of calculating the expected result.
