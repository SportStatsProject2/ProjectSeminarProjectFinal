Underlying Mathematics

This predictor uses a Monte Carlo simulation combined with the Poisson Distribution.

1. Expected Goals (xG) Calculation

First, we calculate the offensive and defensive strength of each team, adjusted by their relative Elo ratings to find the expected goals for the match.

2. Poisson Distribution

Football goals are modeled as discrete events occurring independently at a constant average rate. The probability of scoring k goals is given by:

P(k; λ) = (λ^k * e^-λ) / k!

Where λ (lambda) is the expected goals (xG) for that team.

3. Monte Carlo Simulation

We simulate the match 10,000+ times. In each simulation, we draw a random number of goals for both teams from their respective Poisson distributions. The final probabilities (Win/Draw/Loss) are the frequency of those outcomes across all simulations.
